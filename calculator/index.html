How it works

Swatch in. Yarn mass out.

  1. 1

    Pick a mode — Precise (upload .yy / .zip), Simple (length × width) or Direct area.

  2. 2

    Choose your unit system — grams & centimetres, or ounces & inches.

  3. 3

    Enter your swatch width, height and mass — the bigger the swatch, the better.

  4. 4

    Read your estimated mass, plus a safety range that always adds +10 %.

Quick note: bigger swatches give better estimates. The +10 % margin keeps you safe.
